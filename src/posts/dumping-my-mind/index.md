title: Dumping my mind
date: 2026-11-20T22:39:10+07:00
---

Sometimes there are so many thoughts in my mind, and I let them slowly fall into the void.

But what if I dump them here?

It sounds like an interesting experiment isn't it?

## Internet drama
Drama happens. I get it. At some points I have to come across it, or experience it myself.

What matter is how people involved resolve the issue.

My friend's server, as of writing, is having one. Accusation and such.

As with every situation, context is key. There isn't enough context involved in order to judge.

I wasn't involved, obviously. But any time a drama happens, it affects me. I feel down.

It can be because of my empathy?

I get it, life is not all rosey and rainbows. But unnecessary drama is just that, unnecessary.

It just hurt everyone.

## GenAI
GenAI. GenAI. GenAI. How soon is this hype going to end?

It undermines what it means to engage in creative pursuit.

It enables us to pursue quantity over quality.

It enables us to become more and more detached from reality.

And people who use it, expert or not, cannot escape from its psychological effects on us.

When will people start to value quality software, one that is fast, easy to use, and does not get in the way?

..It can be because I'm in denial. I should embrace the "future". My value doesn't matter.

If only they were true.

## Tired
I feel tired. There are so much work to do and so many things to think about.

And I got so little time for myself.

I guess this is life.

But.. I don't want to be a workaholic. I want to spend more time with myself and my relatives, especially my friends and my family.

Time management sure is one of many important skills in today's world.

..What if there were less things to manage?

Can we apply minimalism to help with this issue?

Guess eventually I could answer this question myself.

## Internal conflict
I have this one feeling toward one of my friend, or actually, my best friend.

The feeling of wanting to spend more time together. To have fun. And to improve.

Is this the feeling that we call.. love?

Or is it just obsession?

I brushed it off though. I just want me and him to remain as bestie.

And it's best for both of us, since we already have partners.

But the feeling remains. And sometimes I cannot stop thinking about it.

Guess I could deal with it somehow.

## Not talking much
I'm usually silent, because I don't know what to say.

It sucks when I talk, though. I stutter. I fumble. I feel anxious.

But then, not talking much causes my English speaking skill to decline.

Or should I say, non-existent?

It sucks.

But I cannot stay here. I need to find ways to make speaking enjoyable, so I don't feel like forcing myself.

I also need to be slow and clear, so people will less likely to misheard.

I can do it.

## Closing
It was nice dumping my mind. My head feels lighter now. And I'm surprised how much I can write.

Is it because writing is easier than speaking? It can be.

But well, these are my thoughts at this time.

See you again soon.
