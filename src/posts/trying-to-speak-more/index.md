---
title: Trying to speak more
date: 2026-11-13T21:55:48+07:00
---

I've been an introvert since high school. It does make some stuff worse, though.

I got into trouble for not speaking up. Many times.

But what could I do? Every time I try to speak up, I stutter. My mind goes blank. My body shakes.

I end up feel bad about myself.

Not speaking often hinders my English skill. I have trouble communicating with my foreign friends on Discord.

And I feel bad about it, again.

...

I cannot let myself be like this forever.

I want to speak more. I want to communicate well. I want to convey my idea.

So that I can have fun with my friends.

So that I can help people.

So that I can show people who I am.

So that I can live well.

A reminder to myself: Try to talk clearly. Try to slow down. Try to step out of your comfort zone.

Try it. Embrace what happens next.

See you.
