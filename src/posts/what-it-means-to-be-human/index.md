---
title: What it means to be human
date: 2026-07-20T15:41:47+07:00
---

We fumble, flop, and fail. We make mistakes. We cause trouble.

But, we can learn. We can grow. We can fix them. We can try our best.

It is what makes human unique and beautiful.

Generate AIs cannot be human, no matter how hard we try.
Although it also fumble, flop, and fail, they cannot 'learn'. They cannot grow.
Because they are, at their core, probabilistic machine.

They are also soulless. Reading a text generated by ChatGPT feels more dull than reading a blog written by a human being.

A text written by a human being has style. It has a soul. It conveys what the writer is thinking. It reflects their experience.

It is what makes a blog worth reading.

Hence, if you use these AIs to write blog for you, then you are not writing.
You are generating noise.

To be human is to think. To reflect. To slow down. To be better.

As soon as you start using these AIs, you stop thinking. You stop creating. You start consuming. Just like how social media and news have been enginnering us to.

But, you can do better.

You can start thinking. You can start creating. You can start failing. You can start being better.

You can start being human.
