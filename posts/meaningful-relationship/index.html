Meaningful relationship

Fri Sep 11 2026

I've met many people in my life. Some of them are classmate, some are roommate, some are now just an acquaintance.

But meaningful? It's rare. Especially nowadays.

It's even harder to find one when I'm socially awkward..

Every time I want to talk, my mind just.. blocked itself. I stutter. I fumble.

I just prefer being a listener and an observer.

But.. when I found that one person who accept me regardless of my trait, I stay with them. I treasure the relationship.

I am happy to be with them. I want to make memorable moments with them. I want to give back.

It's the meaning I've been looking for.

I have one such person, and he's on the internet.

How strange, huh?

But.. that is still good.

When I'm feeling down, he stays with me. He gives me comfort.

He also treasures our relationship as much as I do.

I just feel happy talking with him.

If he's reading this right now, thank you.

Thank you for accepting me. It means a lot to me..

To reader, may this resonate with you.

See you.
