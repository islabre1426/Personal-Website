I'm trying

Sat Oct 03 2026

To be human is to fail. Constantly.

You may fail to live up to your values.

You may fail to be kind to yourself because of it.

You may fail to do anything because of your expectation.

But, better is possible.

As long as you don't give up, you can rise.

Start creating. Start sharing. Start living.

Be kind to yourself, since you can always try again.

Expect less. Go for it. Embrace what happen next.

Rise up.
