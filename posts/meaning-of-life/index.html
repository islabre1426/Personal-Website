Meaning of life

Fri Sep 11 2026

It took me so long to find, but.. I've found the meaning of life.

It's to share, to create, and to live.

And they complement each other.

I live so I can share and create. I create to share. I share and create so I have the will to live.

They fuel each other.

I should've found this sooner, but.. only when life happens that I can find this.

Heh. Now when I think about it, I feel grateful that I can still experience life.

See you.
